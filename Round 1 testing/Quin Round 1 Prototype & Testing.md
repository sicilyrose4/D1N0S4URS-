## The prototype I used to test can be found here: (https://www.figma.com/design/u8s8s1ItabKsIxPMgeFgjR/Bring-People-Together?node-id=1-4)
I tested a clickable Figma prototype of a question app. From the home screen you choose **Ask** (type a question and wait for another person to pick it up) or **Answer** (browse questions other people asked and open a chat with them).

I watched each tester use the prototype and wrote down what they tapped. Afterward I asked them these questions:

1. Was the design easy to understand?
2. Did you get lost at any point while trying to reach your goal?
3. While using the app, did you understand what was going on?
4. Did you understand the purpose of the app?
5. What did you like and dislike?
6. If you could add / change one thing, what would it be?

Testers are listed by first name and last initial because this repo is public.

## Summary

- **Everyone liked the look.** All five said the design was simple and liked the colors.
- **Navigation was the biggest problem.** Most testers tapped "Type here" in the chat expecting to type, and it took them back to the home screen instead.
- **The answer page was hard to use.** The questions felt cluttered, and many looked tappable but weren't. Only the top question opened a chat.
- **What they asked for:** better button navigation (4 of 5), a real typing screen, and more navigation points or pop-ups so you know where you are.

## Notes

### Ben N.
**What they did:** looked at the screen → Ask → typed → tapped "Ask" again → was confused about why they couldn't type → Answer → tapped the top question → back to the chat screen

- **Easy to understand?** "Yeah, very simple, good visual cues, 2 different selections, 'type here'"
- **Got lost?** "I don't think so, was trying to figure out exactly what the buttons did"
- **Understood what was going on?** Couldn't tell exactly what was going on. Clicking "type here" took them back to the home screen, and they were like "what do I do now?"
- **Like / dislike:** Design was simple, liked the colors. The button navigation didn't make sense.
- **Would change:** the button navigation

### Alex K.
**What they did:** looked at the screen → Ask → waited → tapped "type here" → back to home screen → tapped Ask again → chat → back to home → tapped Answer → clicked the top button → chat

- **Easy to understand?** "Very simple, but not always easy. Was kinda confused on where to go"
- **Got lost?** "Yes, I was confused when the chat screen took me back to the home screen"
- **Understood what was going on?** "I understood the purpose, but not always what was going on. I was confused when it took me to a different screen, I was expecting to be able to type"
- **Like / dislike:** "I really liked the simple design and colors. The button nav was confusing"
- **Would change:** "I would add a typing screen"
