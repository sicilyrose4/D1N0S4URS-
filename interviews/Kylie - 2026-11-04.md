# Interview: Kylie — 2026-11-04

- **Who:** Kylie, an undergrad studying speech pathology. Doing a clinical practicum (one individual client and three groups) while applying to grad school.
- **Where / how:** In person
- **Length:** 19 minutes
- **Interviewer:** Sicily
- **Recording / transcript:** Audio and AI transcript saved locally

## Questions to cover

1. **Who they are:** what's their situation, what do they care about?
2. **Their days:** what does a normal day or week look like?
3. **Group activities they're already part of:** clubs, teams, classes, games, group chats, religious or family gatherings… What makes those work (or not)?
4. **How they want to connect with people:** who, how often, in what kind of setting?
5. **What's getting in the way:** time, money, distance, energy, confidence, not knowing where to start…

## What happened

- Spends most of her time on schoolwork, mostly session planning for her practicum: building activities around each client's interests to target their goals.
- Works one-on-one with a client and in three practicum groups, with a faculty member observing at all times.
- Her group splits up who plans which activity right after each session, then relies on a group chat to check in. Everything is due 24 hours ahead, and when others are late, she can't finish her own session plan. At the time of the interview she was the only one in her group with her activity done.
- Motivated by wanting to be successful: getting into grad school, doing well there, getting a good job. Her biggest stress is her upcoming grad school application deadline.
- Looking forward to going home for Thanksgiving to see her family.
- Describes her social role as bringing "fun, go-with-the-flow energy."
- Walked through her day: up at 8, a banana for breakfast and a packed lunch, walked to class in the rain, did homework during class, printed materials at the clinic, worked at the library, another class, more prep, then an individual session at 2:30 and a 90-minute group session (a book club). A quick group meeting at 5:30 to recap and assign roles for next time. Then home, dinner, session notes, a friend stopping by, and bed.
- Spent most of the day around people without talking to them (walking to class, sitting in lectures, the library). She's talking "the entire time" in sessions. At home she talked with her roommates and sister when they were around.
- Says that's a normal weekday. Weekends are less structured and more about friends: going out, watching shows, running errands together (like a Costco trip).
- Her only real free time is Sunday: grocery shopping and prepping for the week, plus a walk or workout when she can. Whether she does those alone or with people depends on whether she feels like she needs to socialize. She always goes grocery shopping with her sister.
- Groups: her practicum groups, a speech club, her friend group, and her family.
- Wishes she talked to her grandparents more. They live in Florida, and she calls them every couple of weeks but feels she should call more.
- Her best get-together was a trip to Vegas: everyone there, high energy, dancing, "no bad vibes," random funny moments.
- Time with people she already knows is easier: "no pressure." She likes meeting new people, but it takes more effort.
- What gets in the way: "I'm just so busy… there's always something I have to be doing."

## Takeaways

- **Relying on others is the hardest part of group work.** Her group divides the work well, but she can't see whether others are on track, and the group chat only lets her nudge. When one person is late, everyone downstream gets stuck.
- **She's around people all day, but rarely connecting.** Lectures, the library and walks to class put her near people without talking to them. Her real connection happens in sessions (where she's "on" professionally), at home with roommates and her sister, and on weekends.
- **Time is what gets in the way.** Her weekdays are fully scheduled, and Sunday is the only free day, mostly spent prepping for the week. Connection gets squeezed into errands she already has to do, like grocery shopping with her sister.
- **The informal moments matter.** The debrief without clients, where her group can relax and laugh, is where they bond. Professional settings don't leave room for that.
- **She chooses social or solo time based on her mood.** Sometimes she needs people, and sometimes she wants to work out alone. Connection works best when it's optional.
- **Familiar people are easier.** Time with people she knows takes no effort and has "no pressure"; new people take work. Her best memories are high-energy, spontaneous times with her friend group.
- **Distance makes keeping up a chore.** She calls her grandparents every couple of weeks and knows she should call more, but it doesn't happen on its own.

## What surprised us

- She was the only person in her group with her part done, and she knew that from the group chat, but there was nothing more she could do than wait.
- How different her group feels with and without clients: professional and "on" in session, laughing together in the debrief.
- Even her free time is mostly prep for the week ahead. Errands double as social time.
- What made her favorite hangout great was everyone's energy ("no one tired") more than the place or the activity.
